1. 🎧 **1 daqiqalik mini-podkast (tabiiy ovozda)**
2. 📝 **Toza konspekt (Markdown formatida)**
3. 🗂️ **Interaktiv Flashcardlar (savol-javoblar)**
yaratib beruvchi Telegram bot.

---

## 🛠️ O'rnatish va Ishga tushirish

1. `.env` faylini oching va 2 ta kalitni kiriting:
   - `BOT_TOKEN` — @BotFather orqali olingan bot tokeni.
   - `GEMINI_API_KEY` — Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com) dan olingan bepul kalit.

2. Botni ishga tushirish:
   - `run_bot.bat` fayliga 2 marta bosing!
